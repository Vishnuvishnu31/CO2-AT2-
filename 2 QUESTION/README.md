Aim


To simulate an AI-based wireless communication network using MATLAB and evaluate the performance of the network based on Prediction Accuracy, Throughput, End-to-End Delay, Resource Allocation Efficiency, and Energy Efficiency. The objective is to analyze how Artificial Intelligence optimizes wireless resource management and improves overall network performance.

Introduction


Artificial Intelligence (AI) has become an essential technology in modern wireless communication systems such as 5G, 6G, IoT, and Smart Networks. AI enables intelligent decision-making by predicting network traffic, allocating resources dynamically, reducing communication delay, and improving energy efficiency.
Traditional wireless systems allocate bandwidth and transmission resources using predefined algorithms. However, these methods cannot efficiently handle dynamic traffic conditions. AI-based optimization techniques such as Machine Learning (ML), Deep Learning (DL), and Reinforcement Learning (RL) continuously learn from network conditions and optimize communication parameters.
In this simulation, MATLAB is used to model an AI-based wireless communication system. The system predicts traffic demand, allocates resources intelligently, minimizes delay, improves throughput, and reduces energy consumption.

Conclusion


The MATLAB simulation demonstrates that AI-based wireless optimization significantly enhances wireless communication performance. By intelligently predicting traffic demand and allocating resources, AI improves prediction accuracy, increases throughput, reduces delay, optimizes resource utilization, and enhances energy efficiency. These improvements make AI-based optimization highly suitable for next-generation wireless networks such as 5G, 6G, IoT, and smart city applications.
